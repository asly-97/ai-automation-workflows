# What is Claude Duet, and how do you try it?

A practical guide to Claude Duet, the background agent Anthropic launched in research preview on October 21, 2026: what it is, how its approval gate works, how to join the waitlist, a safe read-only first job, and the gotchas to check before you trust it with real work. Companion to the Agentic Pod episode "What Is Claude Duet, and How Do You Try It?".

**Watch the episode:** https://youtu.be/eKaObmZXk_Y

Everything below comes from launch-day coverage (linked at the end). Anthropic's own announcement page was not readable when this guide was written, so check it for the latest details, and treat any number here as the coverage's number.

## What Duet is

- A separate app, not a mode inside the main Claude app, in research preview.
- Built on Claude Opus 5.5.
- You talk to it by text or voice in one ongoing thread while it keeps working on a queue in the background.
- It sends a morning brief, checks your connected accounts every few hours, and messages you when something needs a decision.
- It runs on its own cloud computer with its own browser, so work continues when your devices are off.
- Memory, skills and connected apps from your Claude account carry over.
- Connectors: coverage cites 6,000+ connectors and plugins in the Claude Marketplace, with Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe and Shopify named.
- Tasks mentioned in coverage: meeting scheduling, invoice reminders, dashboards, financial or sales forecasts, prototypes and web apps.

## The approval gate

- Duet can read, draft and prepare work on its own.
- Before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything, it shows you the proposed action and waits for your approval.
- Refused even with approval, per coverage: trading securities and moving money between accounts.
- TestingCatalog reports purchases can go through Stripe Link or Shopify Shop Pay, with an approval step first.
- Coverage says action controls use the same classifier as Claude and Claude Code, and that the preview was red-teamed and is covered by a bug bounty. Verify on Anthropic's page.

## How to try it

1. Check you are eligible: US only, Claude Pro or Max. Team and Enterprise are not included yet, with no timeline.
2. Join the waitlist from Claude's own site (look for the Duet page on claude.com).
3. Cost: no separate fee. Background work counts toward your normal Claude usage limits. Exact limits were not published.

## A safe first job

Start read-only and approve every draft yourself:

> Every morning at 7, read my inbox and calendar. Tell me the three things that need a decision and draft replies. Don't send anything.

Run that for a week and judge the morning brief. Then add one small job at a time, for example overdue invoice reminders drafted from your accounting tool, with sending still behind approval.

## Gotchas and limits

- It can misjudge what is urgent or misread a thread (Anthropic acknowledges this, per coverage).
- Personal errands are less developed, and you cannot watch its browser yet.
- Coverage reports no public API and no team mode, so it is one person's assistant for now.
- Access is a waitlist, so you may wait for an invite.
- Background checks use your usage limits, so watch your usage.

## Who it is for

- Good fit: solo operators and small business owners buried in email, invoices and scheduling, especially if you already live in Claude.
- Test first if: you need predictable usage, since exact limits are not published.
- Not for you if: you need a team workspace, an API, or a guarantee that nothing goes wrong.
